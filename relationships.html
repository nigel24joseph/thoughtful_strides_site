Relationship Counselling in Narre Warren

Feel Close to the People You Love

Understand the patterns you bring into relationships, and build the capacity for closer, more secure connection.

Many of the difficulties people experience in relationships have less to do with the other person than with patterns formed long before that relationship began, ways of relating, trusting, and responding to conflict that were shaped early in life and tend to repeat themselves. This individual counselling is for anyone wanting to understand their own part in relationship difficulties, whether they're currently in a relationship, between relationships, or simply noticing a pattern they'd like to change.

This work is separate from couples counselling. It's one-on-one, focused on you, your history, and your patterns, and can be valuable whether or not you have a partner right now.

Signs Relationship Counselling Could Help

  • A pattern of relationships ending the same way, or for similar reasons
  • Difficulty trusting partners, or a fear of being left or betrayed
  • Feeling anxious, preoccupied, or overly reliant on reassurance in relationships
  • Avoiding closeness, or feeling smothered when a relationship becomes serious
  • Repeating dynamics from your family of origin in adult relationships
  • Difficulty communicating needs, or expressing them in ways that push people away
  • Struggling to set boundaries, or feeling responsible for others' emotions
  • Wanting to understand your patterns before entering a new relationship

Understanding Attachment Style

Attachment theory describes how early relationships with caregivers shape the templates we later bring into adult relationships. Someone with a more anxious attachment style may seek frequent reassurance and fear abandonment. Someone with a more avoidant style may feel uneasy with closeness and pull away when a relationship deepens. A more secure attachment style tends to involve comfort with both closeness and independence. Most people show a mix of these patterns rather than fitting neatly into one category, and patterns can look different depending on the relationship.

Importantly, attachment style is not fixed. While early patterns can be persistent, research indicates they can shift over time, particularly through new relational experiences and focused therapeutic work.

The relationships that shaped you early in life do not have to determine the relationships you have from here.

Our Approach

With 20 years of clinical experience and advanced training across trauma-informed and evidence-based modalities, we help you understand where your relational patterns come from and build the capacity to respond differently.

Schema Therapy Addressing early attachment patterns and core beliefs that continue to shape adult relationships.
Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) Identifying and shifting unhelpful relational thoughts and behaviours.
Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) Building tolerance for the discomfort of vulnerability and connection.
EMDR Processing past relational hurt or betrayal, part of our trauma therapy work.
Solution Focused Therapy Practical strategies for communication, boundaries, and self-expression.

What We'll Work On Together

  • Understanding your attachment style and where it comes from
  • Recognising patterns that repeat across different relationships
  • Processing past relational hurt, betrayal, or family of origin dynamics
  • Building healthier communication and boundary setting skills
  • Increasing tolerance for closeness, vulnerability, or independence, whichever feels harder for you
  • Preparing for future relationships with greater self-awareness
