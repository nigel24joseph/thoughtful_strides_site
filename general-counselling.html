General Counselling in Narre Warren

Support for Whatever Life Brings

A safe, confidential space to talk things through, whatever you're facing, and wherever you're starting from.

Not every reason for seeking counselling fits neatly into a diagnosis. Sometimes you're going through a difficult life transition, feeling stuck, overwhelmed, or simply want an experienced, objective person to talk things through with. At Thoughtful Strides Counselling and Psychotherapy, general counselling offers exactly that, a confidential, non-judgemental space tailored to whatever you're bringing to it.

With 20 years of clinical experience across individuals and couples, our approach adapts to you, rather than fitting you into a fixed program. Whether you need a handful of sessions to work through a specific issue, or longer-term, open-ended support, we'll shape the work around your goals, and toward a life that feels more like your own again.

Have a specific question? Jump straight to the FAQs below.

What General Counselling Can Help With

  • Life transitions, career change, relocation, retirement, becoming a parent
  • Stress, overwhelm, or burnout
  • Low self-esteem or confidence
  • Difficult decisions or feeling stuck
  • Life direction, identity, and meaning
  • Relationship or family difficulties
  • Adjustment to illness, injury, or major change
  • General emotional support during a hard period
  • Wanting an objective, confidential space to think out loud

Our Approach

Counselling is, by definition, relational. The quality of the relationship between you and your counsellor is central to how useful the work is. We draw on a range of evidence-based approaches and tailor them to you, so the goal isn't just coping better, it's a life that feels steadier and more genuinely yours.

Core Approaches

Person-Centred Counselling Meeting you where you are, without judgement.
Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) For unhelpful thinking patterns and practical strategies.
Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) Building psychological flexibility and values-based living.
Solution Focused Therapy Practical, goal-directed support for specific issues.

Additional Approaches, Used as Needed

Motivational Interviewing For exploring ambivalence and readiness for change.
Positive Psychology Strengthening wellbeing and resilience, not just addressing problems.
Schema Therapy Where longstanding patterns from earlier in life are part of the picture.
EMDR Where past experiences are still affecting the present, part of our trauma therapy work.

What We'll Work On Together

  • Getting clear on what's actually brought you to counselling
  • Working through a specific decision, transition, or life change
  • Building practical coping strategies for stress and overwhelm
  • Understanding patterns that keep showing up in different areas of your life
  • Rebuilding confidence, direction, or a sense of meaning
  • Shaping a plan that fits your goals, not a fixed program
Counselling is not only for crisis. Sometimes the most valuable work happens when you simply want to understand yourself a little better.

What to Expect

Sessions are 60 minutes, available in person in Narre Warren or via telehealth Australia-wide. Your first session is about understanding what's brought you to counselling and what you'd like to get out of it. There's no set number of sessions, and the pace and focus are shaped around you.
