Support for Whatever Life Brings

A safe, confidential space to talk things through, whatever you're facing, and wherever you're starting from.

Not every reason for seeking counselling fits neatly into a diagnosis. Sometimes you're going through a difficult life transition, feeling stuck, overwhelmed, or simply want an experienced, objective person to talk things through with.

With 20 years of clinical experience across individuals and couples, my approach adapts to you, rather than fitting you into a fixed program. General Counselling and Therapy

Counselling is, by definition, relational. The quality of the relationship between you and your counsellor is central to how useful the work is, so sessions tend to move through a few natural stages as we find our footing together.

1

Understanding What's Brought You Here

We start by getting clear on what's actually brought you to counselling, and what you'd like to get out of it, without needing it to fit a specific diagnosis.

I draw on Person-Centred Counselling to meet you where you are, without judgement, whether that's a life transition, stress, low self-esteem, or simply wanting a confidential space to think out loud.

Read more ↓
2

Understanding the Patterns

Together we work through a specific decision, transition, or life change, and look at patterns that keep showing up in different areas of your life.

Depending on what's most useful, this can draw on Cognitive Behavioural Therapy for unhelpful thinking patterns, Acceptance and Commitment Therapy for psychological flexibility and values-based living, or Schema Therapy where longstanding patterns from earlier in life are part of the picture. Where past experiences are still affecting the present, I also draw on EMDR, part of my trauma therapy work.

Read more ↓
3

Building a Plan That Fits You

We build practical coping strategies for stress and overwhelm, and work toward rebuilding confidence, direction, or a sense of meaning.

I also draw on Solution Focused Therapy for practical, goal-directed support, Motivational Interviewing where ambivalence about change is part of the picture, and Positive Psychology to strengthen wellbeing and resilience, not just address problems. There's no set number of sessions, and the plan is shaped around your goals, not a fixed program.
