Steady Ground to Recover On

I support workers recovering from a work-related psychological or physical injury. I bill WorkSafe directly, so there's no cost to you.

If you've experienced a work-related injury, whether psychological, physical, or both, WorkSafe Victoria can fund counselling to support your recovery and return to work. I'm a registered WorkSafe Victoria counselling provider, offering trauma-informed, evidence-based support to injured workers across Victoria.

Common reasons workers access WorkSafe-funded counselling include workplace bullying or harassment, exposure to a traumatic incident, workplace accidents or injuries, excessive or unsafe workloads, and psychological injury arising from any work-related event or ongoing workplace stressor. Where anger has become part of the picture following a workplace incident, I can also help with anger management counselling as part of this work. Whatever the injury, my goal is the same, steady ground under you again, and a clear path back to work and to yourself.

Am I Eligible?

You may be eligible for WorkSafe-funded counselling if:

  • You are employed in Victoria, or your employment is connected to Victoria
  • You have a physical or mental health condition that was caused, or significantly contributed to, by your work
  • You have lodged, or are about to lodge, a Worker's Injury Claim Form with your employer. If your claim includes a mental injury, treatment can usually start before the claim is accepted (see step 4 below).

How to Access WorkSafe Counselling

1

Report It and See Your GP

Report the injury to your employer in writing as soon as you can, within 30 days. Then see your GP, who can refer you for counselling. If you need time off work, your GP can also issue a Certificate of Capacity. You don't need one to claim treatment costs only.

2

Choose Your Provider

You can choose your own counsellor, as long as they're registered with WorkSafe Victoria.

3

Lodge Your Claim

Complete the Worker's Injury Claim Form and give it to your employer. They complete their section and send it to their WorkSafe agent, who has 28 days to decide your claim. If your injury includes a mental injury, tick the mental injury box.

4

Start Treatment

No prior approval from WorkSafe is needed for counselling. For mental injury claims, provisional payments cover treatment while your claim is assessed, and for up to 13 weeks if it isn't accepted. For physical injury claims, counselling is covered once your claim is accepted.

When the Incident Keeps Replaying

EMDR Therapy for Workplace Injury and Trauma

After a workplace incident, it's common for your mind to keep going back to it. You might replay what happened, feel tense walking into work, or find that a tone of voice or a certain place brings it all back. Even when the physical injury has healed, the psychological impact can make returning to work feel out of reach.

When EMDR may help

  • A traumatic incident at work. An accident, assault, or critical incident that keeps coming back as images, nightmares, or flashbacks.
  • Bullying or harassment. Feeling anxious, small, or on edge long after the behaviour has stopped.
  • Fear of returning to work. Dreading the workplace, certain tasks, or the people involved.
  • Cumulative stress. Frontline or high pressure roles where difficult experiences have built up over time.
  • Painful beliefs since the injury. Thoughts like "I'm not safe", "It was my fault", or "I'm not the person I used to be".

How EMDR helps

EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing) helps process the memory of what happened, so it feels like something in the past rather than an ongoing threat. As the distress settles, it becomes easier to sleep, concentrate, and plan a steady return to work.

You won't need to describe the incident in detail. EMDR can be part of your WorkSafe funded counselling, and we work at a pace that supports your recovery.
