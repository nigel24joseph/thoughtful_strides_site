Depression Counselling in Narre Warren

Reconnect With Life

When everything feels heavier than it should, getting through the day can start to feel like an effort in itself. Depression can show up as a flat, disconnected feeling, or as exhaustion that sleep doesn't fix, or as a quiet sense that the things you once enjoyed have lost their pull. If that sounds familiar, you are not alone, and it is not a sign of weakness. It is a sign that something needs support.

Have a specific question? Jump straight to the FAQs below.

What are the signs of depression?

  • A persistent low or flat mood, most of the day, most days
  • Losing interest or pleasure in things you used to enjoy
  • Fatigue or low energy that doesn't improve with rest
  • Changes in sleep, sleeping far more or far less than usual
  • Changes in appetite or weight
  • Difficulty concentrating or making decisions
  • Feelings of worthlessness, guilt, or being a burden
  • Withdrawing from family, friends, or activities
  • A sense of hopelessness about the future

What Causes Depression?

Depression rarely has a single cause. It often develops through a combination of biological factors, life circumstances, and psychological patterns built up over time. Grief and loss, chronic stress, unresolved trauma, relationship difficulties, major life transitions, and longstanding beliefs about yourself can all play a part. Depression following a significant loss can benefit from grief and loss counselling, depression connected to relationship strain from relationship counselling, and where trauma is underneath it, trauma therapy and EMDR can help.

How Does Therapy Help?

Therapy helps you understand what is driving your depression, rather than just managing the symptoms day to day. Together we look at the thoughts, patterns, and circumstances that keep low mood in place, and build a path toward feeling more like yourself again. This is not about forcing positivity. It is about understanding what is happening and finding a way through it that actually fits your life.

Our Approach

With 20 years of clinical experience, I draw on several evidence-based approaches depending on what fits you best, not a one-size-fits-all program.

Cognitive Behaviour Therapy (CBT) Helps identify and shift the unhelpful thought patterns that feed low mood.
Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) Builds psychological flexibility and reconnects you with what matters to you.
Schema Therapy Addresses longstanding beliefs formed early in life that can quietly keep depression in place.
EMDR Where trauma is a contributing factor, helps process the experiences underneath the depression itself, part of our trauma therapy work.

What We'll Work On Together

  • Understanding what's actually driving your low mood
  • Shifting the thought patterns that keep depression in place
  • Rebuilding motivation and routine, one manageable step at a time
  • Reconnecting with activities and people that used to matter to you
  • Where relevant, processing grief, relationship strain, or past experiences underneath it
  • Building a relapse prevention plan for harder periods ahead
