No Guesswork, Just a Clear Process

Starting therapy can feel like a big step, especially if you don't know what to expect. There is no pressure to have everything figured out before reaching out. Here's exactly what happens, from your first message through to how progress is actually tracked over time.

Getting Started

1

Get in Touch

Reach out via the Contact form, call 0479 171 121, or send an email.

2

Discovery Call

We have a chat so you can talk through your concerns, ask any questions, and see whether I'm the right fit for you. You don't need to commit to anything.

3

First Appointment

Your first session focuses on understanding your experiences, your current concerns, and what you hope to get from therapy. Sessions are available in person in Narre Warren or via telehealth across Australia.

4

Ongoing Support

After your first session, we complete an assessment of your experiences growing up and how they may be affecting you and your current concerns. We then talk through how we'll work together towards what you hope to get from therapy.

How We Track Your Progress

Therapy shouldn't rely on guesswork about whether it's helping. Alongside our conversations, I use validated screening tools before treatment begins and again as we go, so we can both see the trend over time rather than relying on how any one session felt.

DASS-21 Screens for symptoms of depression, anxiety, and stress, used with clients across most areas of practice to establish a baseline and track change.
PCL-5 Screens for symptoms of PTSD, used when trauma is part of the treatment picture, to guide and measure progress in trauma-focused work.
Young Schema Questionnaire Used where Schema Therapy is part of the approach, to identify longstanding patterns and track how they shift over the course of treatment.

These tools aren't about labelling you. They're a shared reference point, completed at the start of treatment and again at intervals, so both of us can see whether things are genuinely shifting, not just guess.
