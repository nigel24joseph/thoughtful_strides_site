No Guesswork, Just a Clear Process

Starting therapy can feel like a big step, especially if you don't know what to expect. There is no pressure to have everything figured out before reaching out. Here's exactly what happens, from your first message through to how progress is actually tracked over time.

What Happens, Step by Step

Before your first session

  1. 1

    Step 1: Get in Touch

    Reach out via the Contact form, call 0479 171 121, or send an email.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Discovery Call

    A free chat so you can talk through your concerns, ask any questions, and see whether I'm the right fit for you. You don't need to commit to anything.

Assessment: sessions 1 and 2

The first two sessions are about understanding you, with questionnaires suited to what you're facing.

  1. 3

    Step 3: First Session

    We talk about what brings you here and what you hope to get from counselling. Sessions are in person in Narre Warren or via telehealth across Australia.

  2. 4

    Step 4: Second Session

    We look at your experiences growing up and how they may be affecting you now, then agree on the treatment approach that suits you best.

Treatment

Depending on what we agree in the assessment, treatment follows one of two paths. Read more about my approach.

Path 1

EMDR

First: safety and stabilisation

We build ways to feel steady and calm yourself, in sessions and in daily life, before any memory processing begins.

Then EMDR processing:

  1. A focused start: two or three EMDR sessions, a day or two apart
  2. Weekly sessions until all the processing is complete
  3. A review of your progress, and support to bring the changes into your everyday life

Path 2

Other Treatment Approaches

Such as Schema Therapy, CBT or ACT, or the Gottman Method and Emotionally Focused Couples Therapy for couples.

  1. Regular sessions working towards the goals we agreed
  2. Your progress reviewed along the way
  3. Support to bring the changes into your everyday life

How We Track Your Progress

Therapy shouldn't rely on guesswork about whether it's helping. Alongside our conversations, I use validated questionnaires at the start and end of therapy, so we can both see what has changed rather than relying on how any one session felt.

Completed during the assessment

Every client

DASS-21

Depression, Anxiety and Stress Scales

21 questions

Asks how often you've noticed common signs of depression, anxiety and stress over the past week. Completed by every client at the start, to set a baseline and track change.

And, depending on what you're facing:

If trauma is identified

PCL-5

PTSD Checklist

20 questions

Each question matches one of the 20 symptoms of PTSD, rated by how much it has bothered you over the past month. Used only when trauma is identified, to guide and measure progress in trauma-focused work.

If you want deeper change

YSQ-R

Young Schema Questionnaire, Revised

116 statements

You rate how well each statement describes you, which shows 20 deep, longstanding patterns called schemas. Used when you want to go deeper in therapy and change these patterns in yourself, and completed again at the end to see how they've shifted.

I use these questionnaires as a reference point. Completing them at the start and end of therapy lets us both see clearly how far you've come.
