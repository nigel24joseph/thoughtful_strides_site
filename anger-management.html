Anger Management Counselling in Narre Warren

Protect What Matters

Understand what's underneath the anger, and build a calmer, more considered way of responding.

Anger is a normal, healthy emotion, but when it shows up too often, too intensely, or in ways that hurt your relationships, work, or wellbeing, it can start to run your life instead of informing it. You're far from the only one who has reached this point. Anger is one of the most common reasons people seek counselling, often after years of managing it alone. We help individuals understand the roots of their anger and develop practical, lasting tools to respond rather than react.

Anger is rarely the whole story. It's often a signal covering hurt, fear, shame, exhaustion, or unmet needs. Our approach looks beneath the surface, so change lasts longer than a set of coping tricks. This isn't about suppressing anger or becoming someone else. It's about being the person you actually want to be in the moment it matters, steady, in control, the version of yourself other people can rely on.

Have a specific question? Jump straight to the FAQs below.

Signs Anger Management Counselling Could Help

  • Frequent irritability, snapping, or losing your temper over small things
  • Angry outbursts you regret afterwards, with partners, children, colleagues, or strangers
  • Feeling like your anger comes on fast and is hard to control once it starts
  • Using anger to avoid feeling more vulnerable emotions
  • Relationship, family, or workplace conflict linked to how you express frustration
  • A history of trauma, high stress upbringing, or unresolved resentment feeding current reactions

Our Approach

With 20 years of clinical experience and advanced training across trauma-informed and evidence-based modalities, we tailor anger management work to what's actually driving your responses, not a one-size-fits-all program.

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) Identifying the thoughts and triggers that escalate anger, and building more helpful response patterns.
Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) Building tolerance for uncomfortable emotions without needing to act on them.
Schema Therapy Addressing longstanding patterns, often rooted in early experiences, that fuel disproportionate anger responses.
EMDR Processing unresolved or traumatic experiences that keep the nervous system on high alert, part of our trauma therapy work.
Motivational Interviewing For those exploring readiness to change entrenched patterns.
Solution Focused Therapy Practical, goal-directed strategies you can apply immediately.

What We'll Work On Together

  • Understanding your personal anger triggers and early warning signs
  • Nervous system regulation, recognising and interrupting escalation before it peaks
  • Identifying what's underneath the anger, whether that's hurt, fear, shame, grief, or unmet needs
  • Practical in-the-moment tools for de-escalation
  • Communication skills for expressing frustration without damaging relationships
  • Where relevant, processing past trauma or experiences that keep anger on a hair trigger
  • Rebuilding trust and repairing relationships affected by past anger
