WorkSafe Victoria Counselling

I'm a registered WorkSafe Victoria counselling provider. I bill WorkSafe directly for your sessions, so there's no cost to you.

To access counselling through WorkSafe Victoria:

  • Report the injury to your employer and lodge a Worker's Injury Claim Form
  • Get a referral for counselling from your GP
  • If your claim includes a mental injury, you can usually start straight away under provisional payments, while your claim is being assessed

No prior approval from WorkSafe is needed for counselling. Sessions are available in person in Narre Warren or via telehealth across Victoria. If you're unsure whether you're eligible, get in touch and I'll be happy to help you work through it. You can read more on my WorkSafe counselling page.
