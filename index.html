EMDR: the foundation of my practice

Many struggles are held in place by negative beliefs formed through earlier experiences. EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing) works with the memories behind them, so they lose their grip and a healthier view of yourself can take their place. That's why I use it across every area of my work.
