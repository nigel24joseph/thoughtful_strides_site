I'm Nigel Joseph, a Trauma Therapist and Couples Counsellor. For 20 years I've worked with individuals, and more recently with couples. I'm EMDR trained, and I work with trauma that shows up as PTSD, anxiety, depression, grief and much more.

Lasting change often comes from understanding the early experiences and relationships that shape how we experience life and connect with others.

EMDR: the foundation of my practice

Many struggles are held in place by negative beliefs formed through earlier experiences. EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitisation and Reprocessing) works with the memories behind them, so they lose their grip and a healthier view of yourself can take their place. That's why I use it across every area of my work, alongside Radical Exposure Tapping (RET) when eye movements don't suit or a more targeted approach helps.
