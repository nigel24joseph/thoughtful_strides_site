My name is Nigel Joseph. I'm a Trauma Therapist, Counsellor and Psychotherapist with 20 years of clinical experience, and I work with people and couples across a wide range of struggles, some loud and disruptive, others quiet and easy to carry alone for years. Whatever brings you here, anxiety, depression, anger, grief, relationship patterns, or a workplace injury, the same trauma-informed approach sits underneath the work, understanding what's driving it, not just managing the symptom on the surface. How I Work

Evidence-based, tailored to you

There is no one-size-fits-all approach to healing. Drawing on EMDR, Schema Therapy, Cognitive Behaviour Therapy, Acceptance and Commitment Therapy, Gottman Method and EFCT-based couples work, I tailor therapy to your specific history and goals, working toward change that's genuinely lasting, not just better coping.
